Overview

Guozhan is a game of hidden loyalties. Every card, General and counter on this page is the real art from the game.

Draft. This is a layout and content draft for review, not the final wiki. Rules marked as pending still need to be written down before they go in.

Every player holds two secret Generals and belongs to the Force of their face-up General. Play cards, use skills and defeat the others until the seats still standing all belong to one Force.

A Game in Four Beats

  1. Setup. You are dealt Generals, pick two and keep them face down. Everyone draws 4 cards.

  2. Turns. Seats take turns counter-clockwise. Each turn runs six phases, and the Play Phase is where you spend cards.

  3. Damage. Cards and skills take away HP. At 0 HP a seat is dying, and any seat may rescue it with a Peach.

  4. Victory. The game ends when every seat still in play shares a Force.

Winning

  • The game ends when every seat still in play belongs to the same Force. Those seats win together.
  • Qun seats never win as a group. A Qun seat wins only when it is the last seat standing.
  • A seat that loses its last HP and is not rescued is out. It discards every card it holds.

Revealing Generals

  • At the start of your own turn, a window lists your hidden Generals and you may reveal any of them, or none.
  • In your own Play Phase a General is revealed only by using something of it that you can use, such as an active skill or a conversion, which reveals it first. You cannot reveal one for no reason.
  • In any phase of any turn, when something happens that a hidden skill answers, such as taking damage or losing equipment, you are asked whether to reveal its General to use the skill. Losing HP never reveals a General by itself.
  • When a seat dies, both its Generals are revealed.

Turn Structure

A turn has six phases, always in the same order.

Start

Start-of-turn skills resolve. You may reveal a General.

Judgment

Each Delayed Tool in your judgment area is judged.

Draw

Draw 2 cards. Some skills change this.

Play

Play cards and use skills. This phase waits for you.

Discard

Keep no more cards than your HP.

End

→
End-of-turn skills resolve. The next seat counter-clockwise begins.

Draw, Discard and the other phases pass by themselves. Only the Play Phase waits for you to end it. A Capture or Starvation can make you skip a phase.

Play Phase

Play cards and use skills in any order. You may use one Sha per turn. End the phase when you are done.

Distance and Range

Who you can reach with a card. Click a seat, then change the table below to see the numbers move.

Distance between two seats is the number of steps the short way around the table, counting only seats still in play. When a seat is knocked out, the seats beside it get closer. It is never below 1.

Distance

  • A Plus Mount on the target adds 1.
  • A Minus Mount on the attacker subtracts 1.
  • Some skills change distance too.

Range

  • Sha can target a seat whose distance is no more than your attack range.
  • Your attack range is your Weapon's range, or 1 with no Weapon.
  • Steal and Starvation can only target a seat at distance 1.
  • Tools that hit everyone, such as Barbarians and Hail of Arrows, ignore distance.

Wuxie on a Tool

Before a Tool takes effect, the game checks for Wuxie. It asks the living seats in seat order, starting from the seat that used the Tool, and only those that hold a Wuxie at that moment. Passing asks the next holder. Passes are not announced, so nobody learns who held one.

Example: Break With a Wuxie Chain

  1. Seat 1 plays Break on Seat 4. The Tool is about to take effect.

    Waiting for a Wuxie check

  2. Seat 2 holds a Wuxie and plays it. Break is cancelled, unless someone counters.

    Break cancelled

  3. The check restarts from the seat after Seat 2, and a seat is never asked about its own card. Seat 3 plays a Wuxie on that Wuxie.

    First Wuxie cancelled

  4. Nobody answers again, so Break goes ahead. Any length of chain works the same way: the last Wuxie decides.

    Break takes effect

Dying and Rescue

When a seat's HP drops to 0 or below it is dying, and a rescue round starts.

  1. Seat 4 takes a Sha at 1 HP and falls to 0 HP. It is now dying.

    Seat 40 HP, dying

  2. The round asks seats in turn order, starting from the seat whose turn it is. Seats without a Peach are skipped, and the dying seat is always asked.

  3. Each Peach heals the dying seat by 1. A dying seat may also play Wine on itself.

    Back Above 0 HP
    The seat is saved and the game goes on.
    Nobody Rescues
    The seat is eliminated and discards every card. Both Generals are revealed, and the killer is rewarded or penalized as under Setup.

Judgment

Turn up the top card of the draw pile to decide an effect.

  1. The top card of the draw pile is turned face up.

  2. Skills that change a judgment can replace the card before it is read.

  3. The final card is read for its suit and rank, and the effect follows.

  4. The card goes to the discard pile.

Delayed Tools

A Delayed Tool sits in a judgment area. It is judged in its holder's Judgment Phase. Wuxie can cancel it when it is judged.

Chains

A chained seat passes fire and thunder damage along to every other chained seat.

Chaining and Unchaining

Play Chains on 1 or 2 seats at any distance, yourself included. Each unchained target becomes chained, and each chained target becomes unchained. You may also recast it: discard it and draw 1 card.

The Chain Wave

Only fire and thunder damage travels. Plain damage never propagates.

Example: 2 Fire Damage on Seat 2

  1. Seats 2, 3 and 5 are chained. Seat 1 uses Fire Attack and Seat 2 takes 2 fire damage.

    Seat 54 HP
    Seat 24 HP
    Seat 34 HP

  2. Seat 2 loses 2 HP and becomes unchained. Its own skills and armor settle first.

    Seat 54 HP
    Seat 22 HP
    Seat 34 HP

  3. The other chained seats are hit one at a time in turn order. Each takes the original 2 damage and becomes unchained. A hit that kills or starts a dying round resolves before the next seat is hit.

    Seat 52 HP
    Seat 22 HP
    Seat 32 HP

Glossary

The terms used across the wiki, each beside the art it refers to.

Force
Wei, Shu, Wu or Qun. You belong to the Force of your face-up General.
HP
Your shared health, built from both Generals.
Main General
The first of your two chosen Generals.
Deputy General
The second chosen General.
Hidden General
A General that is still face down.
Chained
A state that passes fire and thunder damage to other chained seats.
Judgment Area
Where Delayed Tools wait to be judged.
Response
A card played in answer to something another seat did.
Mount
Equipment that changes distance. Plus pushes you away, Minus brings others closer.
Attack Range
How far your Sha reaches. Set by your Weapon, or 1 with none.
Dying
A seat at 0 HP or below, before the rescue round ends.
Draw Pile
The shuffled deck. Judgments flip its top card.
